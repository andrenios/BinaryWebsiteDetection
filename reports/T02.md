## What ran

- `reduce_html` copied unchanged from the SVLM paper's `html_truncation_github.py` (`make_token_counter("gpt-4")` kept). Group order `page_url, signals, forms, hosts, meta, scripts, visible_text` verified by a unit test.
- State builder adds `registrable_domain` (PSL incl. private suffixes, offline; D5) and `favicon_host`, strips `_truncated`, and assembles S0, S1 (1,000), S1-b (100/250/500/1,000/2,000), S2 (S1 + `screenshot_text`), S3 (`page_url` + JCP tag-based truncation to 30k tokens, copied verbatim from `sbaresearch/benchmarking-SLMs`; D10).
- Tesseract 5.5.3 (`eng` only) on the Putra screenshots (`index_js_on.jpg`, D4).
- States written to `data/derived/states/<dataset>/<variant>/<id>.json`; per-site stage wall time to `results/timing/t02_<dataset>.csv`.
- Datasets: `fixture` (20 Putra sites), `bootstrap-putra` (50), `bootstrap-phreshphish` (200, no screenshots). All are development samples; the tables below describe the pipeline (token sizes, truncation, seconds), not detection.

## Observations

- The summariser's budget check does not see the two derived fields added afterwards, so S1-b states can exceed the nominal budget by the size of `registrable_domain` + `favicon_host` (max 1,048 tokens at budget 1,000; 2,048 at 2,000). Kept as is: the budget is defined on the summariser output, as in the SVLM paper.
- At 1,000 tokens 60–69 % of pages are truncated (visible text trimmed); at 2,000 still 32–51 %. The budget sweep (T06b) therefore has real spread.
- S3 states reach 30–32k tiktoken tokens, which is at the API's documented 32k state+question limit; the largest ones may be rejected and must be counted as HTTP failures in T06.
- Summarisation is cheap (≈ 15–30 ms per site for S1) compared with the Jev call (≈ 250–280 ms); S3 truncation is the only expensive stage (quadratic trimming loop on large pages).
