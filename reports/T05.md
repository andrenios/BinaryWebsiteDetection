## What ran

50 balanced bootstrap PhreshPhish sites (25 phishing, 25 benign, S1 at 1,000 tokens), chosen with `SEED=2107`. Part A: `q_direct` alone, three repeated live requests per site with the cache bypassed (150 calls). Part B: one request per site with `q_direct` + `q_direct_choice` + `q_direct_score` (50 calls). Raw responses: `data/raw_responses/t05_light_bootstrap-phreshphish_S1.jsonl`. Results: `results/t05_light_bootstrap-phreshphish_S1_{repeats,structure,summary}.csv`.

**These are bootstrap-sample numbers: they characterise the API's behaviour, not detection, and are not paper material.**

## A. Determinism (three repeats of `q_direct`)

| metric | value |
|---|---|
| sites with three complete repeats | 50 / 50 |
| share of sites with identical probability in all three | 0.38 |
| share with max pairwise difference ≤ 0.01 | 0.48 |
| mean absolute pairwise difference | 0.010 |
| max absolute pairwise difference | 0.08 |
| share of sites whose 0.5-threshold decision flips across repeats | 0.00 |

Probabilities are returned with two decimals; repeated calls differ by one or a few hundredths on about half the sites and never cross 0.5 in this sample.

## B. Structure (`q_direct` vs choice vs score, same state)

| comparison | value |
|---|---|
| agreement `q_direct` > 0.5 vs `choice == phishing` | 0.96 |
| agreement `q_direct` > 0.5 vs `score ≥ 2` (moderate or above) | 0.96 |
| Spearman `q_direct` vs P(phishing) of the choice | 0.94 |
| Spearman `q_direct` vs score expectation | 0.98 |
| Spearman choice P(phishing) vs score | 0.93 |
| mean abs. difference of `q_direct` alone vs with the two companions | 0.007 |
| Spearman `q_direct` alone vs with companions | 0.995 |

(`q_direct` "alone" is the mean of the three repeats.) Companion questions in the same request do not move the `q_direct` probability beyond repeat noise, consistent with the documentation's claim that questions are evaluated independently.
