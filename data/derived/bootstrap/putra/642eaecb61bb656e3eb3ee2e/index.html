THE ABSTRACT STORY

After the battle of the multiverse finished, the Metaverse was born. One of the worlds that remained was invaded by Abstractium Symbiosis. Abstractium invaded all the creative minds, while using anything physical & digital as the host to survive. No place was safe, as Abstractium continued to grow. The entire world essentially became a host for Abstractium symbiosis.

As time passed, creators, collectors, and everyone in between united forces on this redefined world, accepting the abstract ways and building with it. Not only did it bring new light and inspire the citizens to create, but the world thrived. This is when the Abstractars was born, 9001 hosts accepting the symbiosis, and eventually becoming one with their body & mind.

The community flourished, the economy was strong, and the Abstractars continued to develop. Technology thrived and the foundations were being built to accept this new world and share with other unknown life forces in the Metaverse.

Lots of worlds still need to be rebuilt, but there is hope, as the Abstractars will be there to assist, inspire and create as we move forward, united.

THE CHROMA WORLD MAP

SEASON ONE

EPISODE 01:

Abstractium Symbiosis has begun, and the 9001 Abstractars take over Chroma World on the Ethereum blockchain. Over 300 hand drawn traits are used to generate the first complete abstract PFP collection. The ERC 721 collection has some limited 1 of 1 Iconic pieces hidden within. This unique style of NFT art is also your ticket to the full Chroma World experience.

EPISODE 02:

Launch the Chroma World Feature Program. We want to connect & support other creators from across the globe, helping grow a united community. Chroma World will begin supporting specific people in the community. This will be done by having a basic overview of who they are, what they do & showcasing that to the community. We plan on collecting physical & digital work from the feature creators to then give back to community members.

EPISODE 03:

It’s time to get random! Chroma World Randomz Collection is deployed. Unique and random pieces of art will begin to be provided to Abstractar holders. Working our way in a random order from 1 to 9001. The collection & pieces will come in a variety of styles, think art, but no rules.

You can keep your Random, sell it as a secondary within the collection, or even trade with other community members, not financial advice. Some of the pieces will be 1 of 1’s, some collaborations with other creators, and some multiple editions. There is no timeframe on when the entire collection will be complete as it can forever be random and change.

EPISODE 04:

Chroma World Loyalties & Rewards Program is deployed. This is about rewarding the loyal community members & those who HODL Abstractars. The finer details of this will be explained shortly after we launch the collection.

EPISODE 05:

We will continue to grow Chroma World by collaborating with other communities, ongoing marketing, sponsorships etc. The goal is to make Chroma World something that everyone is proud to be apart of. The Metaverse isn’t ready for Abstractium Symbiosis.

EPISODE 06:

We want to bridge the gap between Digital & Physical art, and we aim to do this in many ways. For example acrylic & oil paintings, canvas prints of your Abstractar or Randomz piece, merchandise and apparel. This will be an extension to the Loyalties & Rewards program.

EPISODE 07:

Continue to grow the Chroma World team, so we can become ‘Abstract3D’. This will include a more ‘detail3d’ digital version of your Abstractar, as well as a physical Chroma World collectible.

SEASON ONE SUMMARY

Chroma World Season One will have spin off episodes, not limiting it to what’s above, this will include lots of other little gems, competitions, giveaways, etc.

Updates will be announced via the official Chroma World social media and discord/telegram servers.

Season Two and beyond will be open to all ideas as the team & community expands. Feedback from the community will be essential as we move forward and integrate into the Metaverse.

The space is evolving rapidly, and the above episode sequence could change, or be adjusted to suit the ever evolving world.
