Nunholy is a roguelike action video game developed and published by Chowbie.

About

Nunholy is a nun action roguelike game where you explore dungeons and hunt vampires.

In a world overrun by vampires, nuns with their own stories have arrived at the Hunter’s Guild. They are prepared to borrow even the power of demons if it means defeating their enemies. What fate awaits them?

As players defeat vampires, they can choose to absorb their power or sell it to upgrade their equipment. Whether to borrow the strength of vampires or rely solely on human abilities in battle is up to you.

Vampires are classified into various ranks based on their strength, such as Knight, Baron, Viscount, and Duke. Hunt down the strongest vampires and strive to become an S-rank hunter.

In the dungeons, you will find merchants such as witches, prisoners, and undertakers who don’t care about your identity as long as you have money. Meet and trade with them.
