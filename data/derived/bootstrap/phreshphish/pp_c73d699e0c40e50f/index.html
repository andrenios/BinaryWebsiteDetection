Using MetaMask Wallet

. View Your Assets

The main dashboard displays your Ethereum balance and any tokens you hold. You can add new tokens by clicking "Add Token" and entering the token’s contract address, symbol, and decimal places.

. Send Cryptocurrency

To send cryptocurrency, click the "Send" button. Enter the recipient’s address, specify the amount you want to send, and confirm the transaction. Double-check the recipient address to ensure accuracy.

. Receive Cryptocurrency

To receive cryptocurrency, click the "Receive" button to view your wallet address. Share this address with the sender to receive funds. You can also use the QR code for easy sharing.

. Interact with dApps

MetaMask allows you to interact with decentralized applications directly from your browser. Visit dApp directories like DappRadar or directly connect to dApps through their websites. When you attempt to use a dApp, MetaMask will prompt you to connect your wallet and authorize transactions.

. Swap Tokens

MetaMask includes a built-in feature for swapping tokens directly within your wallet. Select the tokens you want to swap, review the conversion rate, and confirm the transaction. This feature makes it easy to exchange tokens without leaving the MetaMask interface.

Security Best Practices

. Secure Your Seed Phrase

Your seed phrase is the key to your wallet. Store it offline in a secure location such as a safe or a secure password manager. Never share your seed phrase with anyone.

. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Although MetaMask does not support native two-factor authentication, you can enhance security by using hardware wallets such as Trezor or Ledger in conjunction with MetaMask. These hardware wallets provide an additional layer of security.

. Regularly Update MetaMask

Keep your MetaMask extension updated to the latest version to benefit from the latest security features and improvements. Updates are essential for maintaining the security and functionality of your wallet.

. Beware of Phishing Attacks

Always double-check URLs to ensure you are visiting the official MetaMask website or dApps. Be cautious of phishing attempts and never enter your seed phrase on any website or app other than MetaMask. Bookmark important sites and use official links provided by trusted sources.
