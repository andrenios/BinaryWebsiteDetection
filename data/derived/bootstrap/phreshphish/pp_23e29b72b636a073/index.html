  • LCMS Church, School, & Worker Locator Tool

 

From Minneapolis

  • Take 35W to Burnsville.

  • Take Exit 1, which is County Road 42.

  • Turn left (east) on County Road 42.  Continue through two stoplights until you pass under the 35E bridge and come to a third stoplight (exit ramp for 35E).

  • Go to the very next street, which is Grand Avenue (frontage road for 35E); turn right then make an immediate left (you will see our brown brick building).

  • Turn right into our upper lot; this is the main entrance.

From St. Paul

  • Take 35E south to Burnsville.

  • Take Exit 88B, which is County Road 42.

  • Turn left (east) onto County Road 42 and go under 35E.  You will come to a stoplight (exit ramp for 35E).

  • Go to the very next street which is Grand Avenue (frontage road for 35E) and turn right; then make an immediate left (you will see our brown brick building).

  • Turn right into our upper lot; this is the main entrance.

From the South

  • Take 35 north to 35E north to Burnsville. 

  • Exit on County Road 42.

  • Go east on County Road 42 (right) to the very next street, which is Grand Avenue South (frontage road for 35E). 

  • Turn right onto Grand Avenue, and then an immediate left (you will see our brown brick building).

  • Turn right into our upper lot; this is the main entrance.

If you miss Grand Avenue you will come to Plymouth Avenue. Turn right onto Plymouth Avenue and then right again into the parking lot of Good Shepherd Lutheran Church and School.  Proceed slowly through the parking lot of Good Shepherd until you come to our brown brick office building and park in the upper lot. When school is in session (8:15 am – 3:00 pm), please use the instructions below.

When Good Shepherd School is in Session
(8 am - 3 pm)

After turning on Plymouth Avenue, go past Good Shepherd’s parking lot. Continue on Plymouth Ave/145th Street, going around the back of Good Shepherd Lutheran School. Turn right on Grand Avenue South and right again after Havenwood Senior Living to enter our parking lot.

To leave, you will need to go back the same way when the traffic cones are in place. Otherwise, you can proceed slowly through Good Shepherd’s parking lot.
