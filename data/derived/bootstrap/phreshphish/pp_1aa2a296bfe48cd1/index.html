Customer Satisfaction

More Than Just a Great Library System

5 Star Support

What truly sets Accessit apart is our dedicated, worldwide support team. Comprised of experienced school librarians, our experts are ready to assist you, no matter where you are. With Accessit, you don’t just get a software package – you gain a trusted partner committed to the success of your library.

Epic Integrations

Accessit Library is designed to connect directly with the essential systems you rely on every day – from SSO and Student Management, to online content platforms. Through powerful APIs and data-bridges, we ensure a frictionless flow of information across your tech stack.

Easy to Use

We’ve made Accessit intuitive and easy to use for everyone. Beneath its modern interface this system gives you easy access to more resources and information than ever before. Students and staff love being able to access (or manage) their library when and where they want on their own device.
