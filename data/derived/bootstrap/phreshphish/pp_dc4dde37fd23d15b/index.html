A cookie is a small text file that is placed on your device by a website server and which can then be read by the website server that placed it. Cookies allow websites to remember snippets of information. They can be used to enhance your experience on a website, for example by keeping you logged in when you navigate away from a website or keeping track of your shopping basket as you shop. They can also be used to provide useful information to the website owner, such as remembering pages you have visited in order to serve personalised content or advertisements. Information collected by these cookies is aggregated for statistical purposes and does not contain any personal data. For example: • Google Analytics – we use cookies for the Google Universal Analytics service (these may be called “_ga”, “_gali”, “_gat” and “_gid”). We use this information to compile analytics reports and to help us improve the website.

These cookies are used to track your browsing behaviour and to display targeted advertisements to you on other websites based on the content you view on our site. The information collected by these cookies may be shared with third parties such as advertisers and they may use this to build a profile of your interests for the purposes of targeted advertisements. If you disable these cookies you will see generic ads rather than personalised ads on other sites. For example: • Facebook – we use a cookie which lets Facebook know what content you have viewed on our site, in order to display personalised ads to you on the Facebook platform. • YouTube – where we have YouTube videos embedded on our website, cookies may be placed which tracking your viewing activity in order to serve personalised ads or recommend other videos to you.
