My Account

My Account ... the Better Way to Manage Your NJNG Account 

Your customer online portal transforms the self-service experience to make interactions with us easy, personalized and meaningful. Make payments on the go, compare and analyze your natural gas usage, submit and track service requests and more. And that’s just the beginning. Log in or register and start managing your NJNG account like a pro!
