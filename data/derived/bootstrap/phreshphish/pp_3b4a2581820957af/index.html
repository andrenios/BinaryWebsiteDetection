North Carolina Sales Tax:
4.75%
Average Sales Tax (With Local):
6.972%

North Carolina has state sales tax of 4.75%, and allows local governments to collect a local option sales tax of up to 2.75%. There are a total of 460 local tax jurisdictions across the state, collecting an average local tax of 2.222%. Click here for a larger sales tax map, or here for a sales tax table.

Combined with the state sales tax, the highest sales tax rate in North Carolina is 7.5% in the cities of Raleigh, Durham, Durham, Chapel Hill and Wake Forest (and fifteen other cities).
